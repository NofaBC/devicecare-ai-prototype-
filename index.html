Knowledge base & operational boundaries

What the prototype can and cannot claim

Public-document demonstration

The OPTIMIZER Smart Mini A27 scenario is identified as a “Call Doctor” alert. This demo intentionally does not assign a root cause, suggest a reset, establish therapy status, or estimate remaining implanted-device charge. Product-specific documentation must be reviewed and approved by qualified personnel before any real patient-facing deployment.

Included in the single-file prototype

  • Judy-branded scripted chat with interactive questions and safety branching.
  • Optional local ID-card image preview without OCR, submission or storage.
  • Level 1 informational workflow and bounded Level 2 intake.
  • Level 3 ticket simulation with acknowledgement and resolution states.
  • Downloadable synthetic case report; no external services.

Excluded until production authorization

  • Autonomous clinical triage or device diagnosis.
  • Access to implant telemetry, battery readings, programmer or charging controls.
  • Real patient ID OCR, cloud storage and PHI transfer.
  • Live Slack integration, live Judy LLM/voice or manufacturer support connectivity.

Source for future review: Impulse Dynamics technical documents. The demo content is not a substitute for the applicable manual, treating team, or manufacturer.
